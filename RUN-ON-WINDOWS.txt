20. dc-yqq0.25 (final Release on a foreign thread -> owner-thread multi-producer termination queue):
    `RuntimeObjectOwner` now holds a shared `Arc<OwnerTerminationChannel>` with an atomic
    `has_pending` fast-path flag (checked without locking at statement boundaries) and a
    mutex-guarded multi-producer queue (`pending`, `parked`, `pending_drops`); `CompatObjectBase`
    lifecycle flags are `AtomicBool`. A final `Release` on a foreign thread never runs
    `Class_Terminate` and never drops fields/`native_state` on the foreign thread: terminating
    objects park on the owner's queue and non-terminating objects defer to `pending_drops` on the
    owner's queue, draining on the owner thread at the next statement boundary (cascading child
    `Class_Terminate` on the owner thread too).
